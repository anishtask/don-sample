It’s Not Working

How often does it feel like your world’s on repeat?

Another holiday, birthday, holiday again.

Then suddenly you’re wondering where the time (and your life) went?

That’s called aimlessness. And it’s more common than you think.

You’ve Been On Autopilot

Autopilot asks nothing while stealing everything.

It promises comfort while delivering apathy.

It drains your energy and leaves you blaming everyone else for your impotence.

What is The Clarifier? ↗

Until You Begin to Pay Attention

When you slow down, look around, activate your senses, and perceive what is – a whole new world opens up.

When we pay attention, the Universe speaks more clearly, and as a result, we can act more intelligently.

About Don

Bio

Don Rauzelle is a writer, traveler, and eclipse chaser exploring what’s possible when we start paying attention to our everyday lives. He publishes weekly from places like Colombia, Vietnam, and Norway, and his work spans modern parables, long-form essays, short stories, poetry, and visual media, all rooted in the belief that extraordinary insights hide in ordinary moments.

For nearly two decades he worked at the intersection of organizational life, human behavior, and performance inside companies including Google, Adobe, and Electronic Arts. He’s also worked from four continents and is a committed International Dark Sky enthusiast.
